# Open roles

- **Interview coordinator**: find guests, prepare the questions with them, schedule the talk, hand the recording over to editing.
- **Interview host**: lead the conversation; business curiosity matters more than technical depth.
- **Video editor**: cut interviews into a full episode, short clips and a transcript.
- **Reading session host**: pick one or two recent articles every two weeks and moderate the discussion.
- **Technical advisor**: experienced engineer based in Europe who sanity-checks what we publish.
