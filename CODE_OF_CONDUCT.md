# Code of Conduct

> **Draft** — to be confirmed by the management team.

Deploy@EU is a place where practitioners share honestly what worked and what didn't. To keep it that way:

1. **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
2. **Be honest.** Don't fabricate cases, numbers, evidence or contributions.
3. **Protect others' data.** Never share personal data, client information or anything you were given in confidence.
4. **No selling.** Don't use the community to advertise products or services or to collect leads. Members may of course talk to each other privately.
5. **Credit others.** Name the people whose work you build on.

**Reporting:** contact any member of the management team privately. Reports are handled confidentially; people involved step aside.

**Consequences:** a private reminder first; in serious or repeated cases, removal of contributions or badges, and exclusion from community spaces.
