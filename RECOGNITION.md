# Recognition — how contributions are credited

> **Draft v0.2 (2026-11-15).** Open for comments from the management team before it goes live. Badge names and conditions may change.

**In one sentence:** everything you contribute is recorded privately; publicly you show badges you choose (up to five) — no points, no rankings.

## Layers

| Layer | What it is | Public? |
|---|---|---|
| Record | Contribution log: who, when, what, evidence, confirmed by | No — only you and the management team |
| Badges | Earned by doing things; no hierarchy; repeats shown as ×N | You choose up to 5 to show |
| Roles | Long-term responsibilities (e.g. interview lead), with a term | Yes |
| Honours | Sharer of the month, featured case of the quarter, annual thanks — nominated by others, with reasons | Yes |
| Status | Member · Contributor · Management team · Emeritus | Yes |

## What counts as a contribution

| Area | Contribution | Recorded when |
|---|---|---|
| Content | Interview host / interviewer · Interview guest · Case · Video editing · Article · Talk at a session | Interview recorded · case merged · video/article published · talk given |
| Organising | Reading session host · Event organiser · Operations (accounts, groups, notes; monthly) · Mentoring | Session/event held · each month of operations · mentee makes a first contribution |
| Connecting | Bringing a guest or company · Sponsor liaison · Introducing a new member | Guest appears / exchange held (both sides agree to be public) · sponsorship arrives · person joins |

Not counted: number of messages, check-ins, time online, likes, GitHub stars. Business deals between members are not recorded — the community only records the introduction.

## Badges (first set)

| Badge | How to earn |
|---|---|
| 🌟 First Contribution | Your first confirmed contribution |
| 🎙 Interview Pro | Hosted or conducted 3 interviews |
| 🪑 Featured Guest | Were an interview guest |
| 📝 Case Author | A case merged (×N) |
| ⭐ Featured Author | A case chosen as featured case of the quarter |
| 🔍 Case Reviewer | Reviewing cases (role badge) |
| 🎬 Video Editor | Edited a published video or clip |
| ✍️ Writer | Published an article |
| 💡 Speaker | Gave a talk at a session |
| 📚 Reading Host | Hosted a reading session |
| 🧊 Icebreaker | Organised the first of a kind (first offline event, first city, first English session …) |
| 🛠 Backstage Hero | Three months of behind-the-scenes work (operations, notes) |
| 🧭 Mentor | Someone you mentored made a first contribution |
| 🐎 Talent Scout | Someone you introduced made a contribution |
| 🌉 Bridge Builder | Brought a company guest or set up a company exchange |
| 🤝 Sponsor Liaison | Brought a sponsorship |
| 💎 Best Teammate | Nominated by someone you worked with |
| 🌱 Founding Member | One-time: the founding management team |
| 🎖 Current / Former · role | While holding a role / after it |

Badges are proposed monthly from the record (AI-assisted) and confirmed by the recognition coordinator. Nobody confirms their own entries: the coordinator's own entries are confirmed by another management team member.

The first round of badges is backfilled for work already done since the community started, plus the 🌱 Founding Member badge. *Sharer of the month* starts in the month of the first reading session.

## Roles
Roles are the long-term responsibilities listed in the meeting notes (interview lead, reading-session host, accounts, case library, sponsor liaison …). Someone is nominated (self-nomination is fine — a role is work, not an honour), the nomination is announced for 3 days, and if the management team has no objection the term starts. Terms are 6 months and renewable; newcomers can shadow the current holder first. Roles do the work; decisions on money and direction stay with the management team. No growth targets are set for volunteers.

## Honours
| Honour | How often | Nominated by | Confirmed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sharer of the month | Monthly | Any member (not yourself) | Management team |
| Featured case | Quarterly | Reviewers and management team | Management team |
| Annual thanks | Yearly | Management team | Management team |

We honour "the most helpful contribution", not "the most contributions". Honours come with no money, gifts or sponsor benefits. Management team members do not compete for honours.

## Removal and appeals
Entries and badges can be removed for fabricated evidence, gaming, or serious breaches of the Code of Conduct — after a private reminder. People involved step aside from decisions about themselves. Appeals go to any management team member not involved, within two weeks.

## Privacy
Only you and the management team see your full record. Public are your name, your credited work, and the badges, roles and honours you agree to show. You can request your record or its deletion at any time. AI tools that help prepare monthly summaries are named in this document once chosen.

## Changing these rules
Changes are proposed by the recognition coordinator and announced in the community chat one week in advance; if nobody objects, they take effect. We review once a quarter and change at most one or two things at a time.
