# Governance — Deploy@EU Community Principles

> **Draft (2026-11-09).** To be confirmed by the management team. Once in force, any change needs the explicit agreement of the whole current management team.

These principles only state principles. Where they are silent, we decide by the principles and then add the decision here.

## 1. Who we are
We advance the adoption of AI and digitalisation in European companies. At the core are the people who actually do the deployment work; around them we connect companies, newcomers who want to enter the field, and partners. We work mainly in English and local languages. We focus first on the platform and content; events and collaborations follow.

## 2. Non-profit
The community is a non-profit organisation.
1. No membership fees.
2. Money the community receives is used only for the community itself, e.g. running events and paying people who work for the community.
3. The community's own talks and courses are free.
4. The community does not endorse any company — including sponsors and members' own companies: it does not promote their products or courses and takes no commission. It may showcase members' practice; sponsors may be credited at the events they support.
5. Collaborations between members are outside the community; the community is not involved and takes no commission.

## 3. Members and the management team
1. For now there are only members and the management team.
2. The management team decides three kinds of matters: the community's direction; how the community's money and resources are used; who joins the management team.
3. Being on the management team is a responsibility, not a power.
4. Joining the management team: having already completed concrete work with the community, being willing to commit long-term, and the explicit agreement of the whole current management team.
5. Leaving the management team: on one's own request. Those who leave keep an honorary status and do not become ordinary members again.
6. While the management team has fewer than 5 people, the matters in point 2 require the explicit agreement of the whole current management team.
7. Decisions of the management team are recorded in a shared document.
8. Every account and asset of the community (groups, documents and folders, social accounts, email, domain, this GitHub organisation) has at least two administrators.

## 4. Changes
Once in force, any change to these principles requires the explicit agreement of the whole current management team.
