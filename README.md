# Deploy@EU Case Library

**Real stories of putting AI to work in European companies — written by the people who did it.**

Deploy@EU is a non-profit community of practitioners who deploy AI and automation inside European businesses: forward-deployed engineers (FDEs), in-house builders, consultants and the business owners who work with them. This repository is our shared library of deployment cases.

> **Status:** early stage. The rules below are drafts and the first cases are being written — yours could be one of them.

**Website:** https://deploy-eu.github.io · **Write a case with your AI:** copy *"Help me write a Deploy@EU case and member profile: https://deploy-eu.github.io/write-a-case.txt"* into your assistant.

## What you will find here

Each case answers the questions practitioners actually ask each other:

- What was the business problem, in which industry and country?
- Which systems were already there, and which AI tools were added?
- What does the workflow look like now?
- What changed — measured, not guessed?
- **Where did it get stuck?** Legacy systems without an API, compliance, people, data quality …
- What would you do differently?

Browse them in [`cases/`](cases/) (one folder per author) or on the website: https://deploy-eu.github.io/#cases

## Why share a case

- **Your name stays on your work.** Every case is credited to its authors and linked to their profiles (see [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md)).
- **It helps the next person.** The same walls get hit again and again. A short, honest case saves someone else weeks.
- **It is a portfolio you can point to.** A reviewed case with your name on it says more than a badge.

## How to contribute

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). In short:

1. Copy [`cases/_TEMPLATE.md`](cases/_TEMPLATE.md) and fill it in, or open a **Case submission** issue if you don't use Git.
2. A maintainer reviews it (anonymisation, consent, honest numbers, no advertising).
3. Once merged, you are added to the contributors list.

Not ready to write a whole case? You can also recommend an interview guest, fix or complete an existing case, or tell us about a wall you hit — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#first-contributions).

## Our rules in one paragraph

This is a community space, not a marketplace. We do not endorse any company — not sponsors, not our members' employers or businesses. Cases describe what was done and what happened; they are not product pitches, and they contain no prices or sales contacts. Personal and confidential data stay out. Details: [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md), [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), [RECOGNITION.md](RECOGNITION.md).
